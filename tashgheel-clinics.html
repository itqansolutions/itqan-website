Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tashgheel Clinics?

Tashgheel Clinics is a clinic management system made by Itqan Solutions in Cairo. It covers patients, appointments, doctors, consultations, stock, payments and reports.

Which clinics is it for?

It is built for clinics with one or more doctors, and for cosmetic and aesthetic centers that run treatment sessions and use consumables.

Does it have an Arabic interface?

Yes. It supports Arabic with right-to-left layout, and English as well.

Can patients book online?

Yes. There is a public booking page where patients can request an appointment, and the request reaches your team in the system.

Can it track the stock used in each session?

Yes. You can keep products and vendors, record purchases, and log the items used during a consultation so stock stays accurate.

Can it handle patient payments?

Yes. Reception can collect payments against a visit, and managers can see transactions and the financial picture in the finance and reports screens.

Is patient data protected?

Each clinic has its own separate account space, and staff only see what their role allows. Changes are recorded in an audit log.
