Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tashgheel CRM?

Tashgheel CRM is a cloud CRM and business system made by Itqan Solutions in Cairo. The general edition covers sales, finance, inventory, procurement and HR. A separate real estate edition covers developers and brokers.

Is it available in Arabic?

Yes. The interface works in Arabic and English.

What is the difference between the general and the real estate edition?

The real estate edition adds projects, units, reservations, contracts, installments and commissions. The general edition is for companies that sell products or services to customers.

Can it create quotations and invoices?

Yes. You can prepare quotations, convert them to sales orders and issue invoices and receipts.

Does it support Egyptian e-invoicing?

Tashgheel CRM includes e-invoicing integration settings for the Egyptian Tax Authority. Ask us how it fits your tax registration when you request a demo.

Can I connect Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp?

Tashgheel CRM connects to Meta lead forms and WhatsApp. WhatsApp needs a verified Meta business account, and we help you set it up.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It is a web application. Your team signs in from any browser.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is tailored to team size and modules. Request a demo or a quote and we will reply with a clear offer.
