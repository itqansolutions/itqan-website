Built for real estate sales teams

Tashgheel CRM is for companies that sell property and need to see the whole journey from first inquiry to handover.

  • ✓ Real estate developers selling off-plan and ready units
  • ✓ Brokerages and sales agencies with several teams
  • ✓ Sales managers who need targets and follow-up discipline
  • ✓ Owners who want one system for sales and the back office

Key Features

📈

Leads and sales pipeline

Capture leads, assign them to reps and move each deal through a visual pipeline with tasks and follow-ups.

📣

Meta lead forms

Bring leads from Facebook and Instagram lead forms straight into the CRM.

💬

WhatsApp integration

Message leads on WhatsApp from the customer record. Needs a verified Meta business account, and we help set it up.

Projects, phases and units

Reservations and contracts

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tashgheel CRM?

Tashgheel CRM is a cloud CRM and business system made by Itqan Solutions in Cairo. It has a real estate module for developers and brokers, plus sales, finance, inventory, procurement and HR modules.

Is Tashgheel CRM available in Arabic?

Yes. The interface works in Arabic and English, so your team can use whichever language they prefer.

Can it manage installment plans for units?

Yes. You can create installment schedules for each contract and record receipts against them, so you can see what has been paid and what is due.

Can I capture leads from Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Property Finder?

Tashgheel CRM connects to Meta lead forms, WhatsApp and Property Finder. WhatsApp needs a verified Meta business account, and we help you set up each connection.

Does it support Egyptian e-invoicing?

Tashgheel CRM includes e-invoicing integration settings for the Egyptian Tax Authority. Ask us how it fits your tax registration when you request a demo.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It is a web application. Your team signs in from any browser, on the office computer or a phone.

Can brokers and managers have different permissions?

Yes. Users get roles, so a broker sees their own leads while a manager sees teams, targets and reports. Changes are recorded in an audit log.
