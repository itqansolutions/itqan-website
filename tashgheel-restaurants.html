Frequently Asked Questions

What is Tashgheel F&B?

Tashgheel F&B is a restaurant POS and management system made by Itqan Solutions in Cairo. It covers the POS, tables, kitchen screen, delivery, recipes, stock, suppliers and reports.

Which businesses can use it?

Restaurants, cafes, quick-service shops and multi-branch food brands, with dine-in, takeaway and delivery orders.

Can customers order from the table with a QR code?

Yes. QR table ordering can be switched on or off by the admin, and a waiter approves each order before it goes to the kitchen.

Does it calculate food cost?

Yes. You define recipes and ingredients, and the system works out the cost of each item. Stock is deducted when the invoice is closed.

Does it support multiple branches and staff roles?

Yes. You can run several branches, and give cashiers, waiters, kitchen staff and managers different access.

Is there an Arabic interface?

Yes. The system supports Arabic and English.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. You can register on the F&B app and start a free trial, or request a demo and our team will walk you through it.

How much does it cost?

We quote by number of branches and modules, and we do not publish a price list. Request a demo or a quote and we will reply with a clear offer.
